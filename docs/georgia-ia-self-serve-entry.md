For Rolf to approve or correct before Georgia uses it. Wording below is what Georgia would say; the notes in square brackets are for Rolf and are not shown to clients.

## Insurance: what you can do yourself in My Client Space

For any of your iA insurance policies:
- Change your banking information.
- Change your payment date.
- Change your contact information.

For newer ("modernized") policies only (Access Life sold since April 2024, Transition sold since November 24, 2025, and Term and Whole Life sold since March 30, 2026):
- Change how often you pay, or the payment method.
- Delay, stop or restart your payments.
- Change a beneficiary (except an irrevocable one). [Decision for Rolf: should Georgia mention this, or leave beneficiary changes with you? The plan recommends leaving them with you.]

Participating Life and Universal Life policies are not in the newer group, so the second list does not apply to them.

## Insurance: what Rolf does for you

Anything else goes through your advisor: reducing or ending a policy or a coverage, changing the advisor on a policy, changing your name, sex or date of birth, changing coverage, and loans or withdrawals. Georgia passes these to Rolf. [They are done by the advisor in iA's Advisor Centre, by EVO online changes, or on paper forms F30A-215, F4A-01 (with F51-312A for a business), F5A and F6A. Georgia does not name the forms to clients.]

For Accident, Disability and Critical Illness (living benefits) policies, contact changes go to iA at livingbenefits@ia.ca or 1-844-442-4636, and other changes go through Rolf.

## Investments

For now you can make a deposit yourself in My Client Space. iA is adding more online options, and Georgia will not describe any other investment change as available until this entry says so. Everything else (withdrawals, transfers, investment instructions, changes to an investment account's banking details) goes to Rolf. [Decision for Rolf: banking-detail changes on investment accounts, since the investment note covers deposits only.]

## How Georgia uses this

- She decides whether a policy is "newer" from the policy's type and issue date in the CRM. If either is missing, she hands the request to Rolf.
- She says what the client can do and where (My Client Space), never walks through screens she has not been given, and never says a change has been made.
- She never asks for banking details, policy numbers or account numbers in the chat.
- Anything not in this entry goes to Rolf.
