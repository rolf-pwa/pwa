## Where to go

My Client Space is iA's own site for your iA accounts and policies. You will find a link to it in the sidebar of this portal.

If this is your first time there, you will create an account. It asks for a contract number. I can show you yours here in the portal. [Georgia shows it only to a signed-in client, only for their own contract, and only the product they ask about.]

## Insurance: what you can do yourself in My Client Space

For any of your iA insurance policies:
- Change your banking information.
- Change your payment date.
- Change your contact information.

For newer ("modernized") policies only (Access Life sold since April 2024, Transition sold since November 24, 2025, and Term and Whole Life sold since March 30, 2026):
- Change how often you pay, or the payment method.
- Delay, stop or restart your payments.

Participating Life and Universal Life policies are not in the newer group, so the second list does not apply to them.

[Beneficiary changes on newer policies are also self-serve at iA. Georgia does not mention this: she hands beneficiary requests to Rolf. Tell her to mention it and I will add it here.]

## Insurance: what Rolf does for you

Anything else goes through your advisor: reducing or ending a policy or a coverage, changing the advisor on a policy, changing your name, sex or date of birth, changing coverage, changing a beneficiary, and loans. Georgia passes these to Rolf. [They are done by the advisor in iA's Advisor Centre, by EVO online changes, or on paper forms F30A-215, F4A-01 (with F51-312A for a business), F5A and F6A. Georgia does not name the forms to clients.]

For Accident, Disability and Critical Illness (living benefits) policies, contact changes go to iA at livingbenefits@ia.ca or 1-844-442-4636, and other changes go through Rolf.

## Investments

For now you can make a deposit yourself in My Client Space. iA is adding more online options, and Georgia will not describe any other investment change as available until this entry says so.

Withdrawals are never done online by you: they go through Rolf. Changes to the banking details on an investment account also go through Rolf, as do transfers and investment instructions.

## Documents

Georgia never sends a document by email. She tells you which document it is and that you will find it in My Documents in the portal sidebar. If it is not there yet, she tells Rolf so he can add it.

## How Georgia uses this

- She decides whether a policy is "newer" from the policy's type and issue date in the CRM. If either is missing, she hands the request to Rolf.
- She says what the client can do and where (My Client Space), never walks through screens she has not been given, and never says a change has been made.
- She never asks for banking details or account numbers in the chat. She may show a signed-in client their own contract number for registering at My Client Space, as described above.
- Anything not in this entry goes to Rolf.
